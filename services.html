Coaching with noein.

A space to think. A space to understand. A space to become.

I offer one-to-one, 60-minute transformative coaching sessions, creating a calm, supportive and collaborative space for you to reflect and explore what matters most.

Transformative coaching offers you time and space to explore what matters, understand what’s shaping your choices and reconnect with your true self.

Coaching at noein is for individuals and professionals who want to understand themselves more deeply and make choices that feel aligned rather than imposed.

How our sessions work.

  • One-to-one 60-minute coaching sessions
  • Available online or in person
  • Sessions in English and Greek
  • Mostly evenings & weekends
  • Single sessions and blocks of sessions available

We begin with a free 30-minute Discovery Call — a relaxed conversation to explore what you’d like to work on and whether coaching feels right for you.

Who I work with.

At noein, I support individuals and professionals who, like me, feel a pull towards something more aligned – a desire to reconnect with themselves, gain direction and build confidence in the choices ahead.

They may be questioning the path they’re on, struggling to recognise their own worth, or sensing they have more to offer but aren’t quite sure how. Some may feel unseen or unheard, or find it difficult to make their strengths, ideas and perspectives known.

Wherever they find themselves, coaching at noein offers a space to really listen to, understand and acknowledge themselves, helping them make choices and take steps forward in a way that feels more genuine and fulfilling.

My coaching style.

My approach is reflective, calm, and centred around awareness. I meet clients where they are, following their pace, their questions and what naturally arises in the space we create together.

I listen closely to what clients say and how they express themselves, reflecting observations and patterns to help them explore different perspectives. I follow the clients lead while remaining curious to explore the thoughts, assumptions and motivations behind their actions and choices.

I offer a space where clients can feel heard and understood, without judgement, while also gently challenging them to look at things differently. Together, we explore what emerges, allowing for deeper self-understanding and forming a way forward that feels more genuine and their own.

What coaching can help with.

  • Reflect on where you are in your career and explore what comes next
  • Recognise what you bring to your profession and find your place within it
  • Develop a more authentic, self-aware approach to leadership
  • Find clarity when you feel stuck, uncertain or out of place
  • Reconnect with values, strengths and priorities
  • Navigate change or transition
  • Understand the patterns shaping your thoughts, behaviours and choices.
  • Find clarity and make choices with intention
